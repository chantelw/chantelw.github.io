Who We Are

Food.
Community.
Connection.

Peel Food Link is a registered non-profit organization serving Brampton, Mississauga and Caledon through a mobile food support model.

We believe everyone deserves access to nutritious food with dignity, compassion and respect, regardless of their circumstances.

By removing transportation, mobility and accessibility barriers, we connect individuals and families with essential food resources while fostering hope, inclusion and community.

Through dedicated volunteers, strategic partnerships and a commitment to service, Peel Food Link works to ensure that no one is left behind.
