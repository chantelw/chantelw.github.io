# chantelw.github.io
Peel Food Link is a mobile food program that delivers essential food support to individuals facing barriers to accessing traditional food banks. 

Serving residents across Brampton, Mississauga and Caledon, we provide direct-to-door food delivery for people experiencing temporary or permanent disabilities, limited mobility, low income or chronic health conditions.

As a registered non-profit organization, Peel Food Link enhances food security by removing access barriers and ensuring equitable, dignified support for vulnerable populations
